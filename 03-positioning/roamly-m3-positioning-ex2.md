# Roamly Groups · Positioning Framework & Statement, Module 3 (Exercise 2)

## Responses
- **Your final positioning statement:** Roamly Groups is group booking for curated local experiences with vetted hosts: one friend organizing a trip invites the group, and everyone confirms and pays their own share in one shared booking. The group goes from "we should do this" to booked and paid, without one person acting as planner and payment collector.

## Show and swap your statement
- **Who specifically is this for, could you describe the target person in your own words, without using the words in the statement?:** Whoever in a social circle ends up running the logistics of an outing. They message everyone, wait for replies, and get stuck fronting the money. I'm not sure if it's for travelers or locals, or whether it's only for friends. It could be a family, a team, or a club.
- **What value does this deliver, what specifically gets better for the person it’s for?:** Everyone handles their own part of the payment, so the person in charge doesn't get stuck covering the group or chasing people. It gets an outing confirmed quickly. The "curated" and "vetted" part seems to mean the activities are screened, but I couldn't say what that changes for me.
