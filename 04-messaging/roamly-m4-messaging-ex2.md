## Why these surfaces
A surface is a place where a customer meets the message. These three fit a product-led motion, where the invite does the selling.
- **Email subject line:** the invited friend's first touch. It has to earn the open, so it leads with the outcome.
- **In-app notification:** the moment of use. It tells the person what happens next.
- **Social post:** shareable and light on context. It leads with the human outcome and closes with proof. This is the one turned into the visual asset.
- **Left out:** homepage headline (the audience arrives through invites) and sales one-liner (no sales team in this motion).

## Signal and fix
- **Signal:** Audience partly sharp (the person who does the chasing came through, but the travel context was only hinted at). Product partly clear (the payment mechanic came through, but not what the group was booking). Evidence came through with specific numbers.
- **Fix applied:** Named the experience on the card ("Old Town Food Walk, hosted by a vetted local") so the product and the travel context are both visible. The blind read answers above were taken before this fix.
