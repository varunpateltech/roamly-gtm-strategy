# Roamly Groups · Messaging Pillars & AI-Generated Asset, Module 4 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **FROM (Life before):** Group trips run on one person's goodwill. The organizer messages everyone, waits days for replies, puts their own card down, and then chases friends for money. Plans stall in the group chat, and half the ideas never get booked.
- **TO (Life after):** Deciding together and paying individually happen in the same moment. Everyone confirms their own spot and settles their own share, so no single person carries the group, and plans that get agreed on actually happen.
- **Total number of audiences that need their own message:** 2
- **Justification, why does each one need its own pillar?:** Two audiences: the organizer (the friend who plans the trip and starts the booking) and the invited group member (the friend who receives the invite and has to decide whether to join and pay). Role or buying position: the organizer initiates and carries the coordination burden, while the invitee didn't choose the activity and is being asked to commit their money. Journey stage: the organizer is often already a Roamly user, while the invitee is often new to Roamly and hasn't yet decided to trust it. One message fails the test: "Stop chasing people for money" moves the organizer, but it doesn't address what the invited member is deciding, which is what they're paying for and whether it's easy to say yes.

## Audience 1: The organizer
- **Audience 1:** The organizer
- **Value Prop (The promise):** Be the one who makes the trip happen, without carrying the group.
- **Capabilities (The proof points):** Invite the whole group in one step. Keep one shared plan of the group's activities. Each person confirms and pays their own share automatically.
- **Evidence (The receipts):** 12,000+ vetted local experts across 40 cities. Repeat booking rates 3x the industry average.

## Audience 2: The invited group member
- **Audience 2:** The invited group member
- **Value Prop (The promise):** Say yes in seconds and pay only your own share, with your spot locked in.
- **Capabilities (The proof points):** Join straight from the invite. See the plan and your exact share before you confirm. Pay your share in the same step.
- **Evidence (The receipts):** Every experience is hosted by a vetted local expert. Repeat booking rates 3x the industry average.
